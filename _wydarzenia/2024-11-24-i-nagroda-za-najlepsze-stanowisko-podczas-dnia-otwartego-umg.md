text_en: The presentation of the achievements of student research groups is a permanent and crucial part of the Gdynia Maritime University Open Days. During the last edition of the event, a competition for the most interesting presentation was held in the Tadeusz Meissner Auditorium. The jury assessed not only creativity and commitment to promoting the university, but above all, the high scientific level of the presentations. We are pleased to announce that thanks to the passion and professionalism of our student research group members, we won first place in this competition.
