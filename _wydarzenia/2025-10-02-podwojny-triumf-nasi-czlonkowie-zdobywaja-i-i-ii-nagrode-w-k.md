Ponadto Paweł Jędryczka otrzymał nagrodę Ministra Infrastruktury dla najlepszego elektroautomatyka okrętowego Uniwersytetu Morskiego w Gdyni. Serdecznie gratulujemy i życzymy dalszych sukcesów‼!'
text_en: In the 12th edition of the Competition for the Best Engineering Thesis 2025, organized by the Association of Polish Electrical Engineers (SEP), our members Paweł Jędryczka and Kamil Kaczmarek were the winners. Additionally, Paweł Jędryczka received the Minister of Infrastructure Award for the Best Ship Electrical Automation Engineer at the Gdynia Maritime University. Congratulations and we wish you continued success!
