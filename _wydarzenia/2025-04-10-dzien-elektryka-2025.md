text_en: On Thursday, Electrician's Day took place, organized by the Dean and students of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering. Over 300 people attended the event, including students from six high schools, as well as representatives of companies in the energy, electronics, IT, and home automation sectors interested in collaborating with students. Our booth and an escape room prepared by our colleagues from the group were also on hand.
